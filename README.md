# Lightsaber-effect
Lightsaber Effect turns a blue marker into a glowing blade. It uses your webcam to look at every frame, picks out the blue color, and follows the marker’s position. A bright blade with a soft glow is then drawn on top of it. Everything runs in the browser, so all you need is a webcam and a blue marker.

## How to use

1. Allow camera access when the browser asks.
2. Hold a blue marker in front of the camera.
3. Move the marker and the blade follows.

## Controls

Press these keys on your keyboard to change the blade color:

- `1` blue blade
- `2` red blade
- `3` white blade

## Tips

Use good lighting and a background that doesn't match the marker's color. Sound starts after the first click or key press.
